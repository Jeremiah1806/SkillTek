Methodology
Transparent intervention modelling

The simulator does not assume that every training seat becomes an available worker. Instead, it applies a configurable conversion factor to estimate the portion of additional seats that contributes to projected workforce supply. This assumption can be replaced or calibrated as real programme outcome data becomes available.
