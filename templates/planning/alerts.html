How the Early Warning System works

SkillTek compares forecasted labour demand and worker supply at district-occupation level and converts significant deviations into prioritised signals.

Alert severity incorporates projected gap magnitude, model confidence and persistence of the market condition. These signals support planner review and do not replace policy judgement.
